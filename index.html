Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Privacy summary:

GlowSync is designed to work locally on your Windows device. It does not send screen contents, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or telemetry to the developer.

1. Who is responsible for GlowSync

GlowSync is published and maintained by Oliwier Baran.

Privacy contact: oliwier.baran@icloud.com

2. What data GlowSync processes

To provide screen-synchronized ambient lighting, GlowSync may process the following information locally on your device:

  • Screen image data from the display selected by you. Frames are sampled in memory to calculate colors for the monitor lighting.
  • Display and hardware information, such as display resolution, display identifiers, detected HID/LampArray devices, device capabilities and timing information required to communicate with supported lighting hardware.
  • Application settings, such as the selected display, brightness, synchronization preferences, theme-related preferences and start-with-Windows preference.
  • Local diagnostics, such as capture rate, lighting update rate and HID write timing, when displayed by the application for troubleshooting.

3. How the data is used

The information above is used only to provide GlowSync functionality: capture the selected display, calculate ambient-light colors, communicate with compatible lighting hardware, remember user preferences and show local diagnostics.

4. Data collection and transmission

GlowSync does not transmit the processed screen contents or application usage data to the developer. The application does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking, telemetry or crash-reporting services.

Screen frames used for color calculation are processed locally and are not intentionally saved by GlowSync as screenshots or recordings.

5. Storage and retention

Screen image data used for synchronization is transient and processed in memory. User preferences may be stored locally on the Windows device so that settings can be restored between launches. GlowSync does not maintain a developer-operated cloud database of user data.

6. Sharing and recipients

GlowSync does not sell user data and does not share screen contents or usage data with advertisers or data brokers. Local information is handled by the Windows operating system and the hardware interfaces necessary for the application to operate.

If GlowSync is obtained through Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process store, purchase, installation or platform diagnostic information independently under Microsoft's own terms and privacy policy. That processing is not controlled by GlowSync.

7. Security

GlowSync minimizes privacy risk by keeping screen processing and device control local. No developer-operated remote service is required for normal synchronization. Users should keep Windows and GlowSync up to date and protect access to their Windows account and device.

8. Your control over data

You can stop screen processing at any time by stopping synchronization or closing GlowSync. You can change application preferences in GlowSync and can remove locally stored application data by uninstalling the application and, where applicable, deleting remaining local settings.

Because GlowSync does not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database of personal data, the developer generally has no remote user record to access, correct, export or delete. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact address above.

9. Children

GlowSync is a general-purpose Windows utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The application does not create user profiles or transmit screen contents to the developer.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when GlowSync functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or GlowSync privacy practices, contact oliwier.baran@icloud.com.
